# 🧩 STEP 1: Get your Binance ETH deposit address

Inside Binance:

1. Go to **Wallet → Deposit**
2. Select:
    - Coin: **ETH**
    - Network: **Ethereum (ERC20)** ⚠️ IMPORTANT
3. Copy address

👉 This is where your ETH will go

---

# 🧩 STEP 2: Send ETH from Trust Wallet → Binance

In Trust Wallet:

1. Open ETH
2. Click **Send**
3. Paste Binance address
4. Enter amount
5. Confirm

👉 Pay gas fee (₹200–₹1000+)

---

# 🧩 STEP 3: Wait for confirmation

- Takes: **1–5 minutes (sometimes longer)**
- ETH needs confirmations on blockchain

👉 After that → ETH appears in Binance

---

# 🧩 STEP 4: Convert ETH → USDT (IMPORTANT)

Why?  
👉 P2P mostly works with **USDT**

Steps:

1. Go to **Trade → Spot**
2. Select pair: **ETH/USDT**
3. Sell ETH → get USDT

👉 Small fee (~0.1%)

---

# 🧩 STEP 5: Move USDT to Funding Wallet

This step beginners miss:

1. Go to **Wallet → Transfer**
2. From: Spot Wallet
3. To: Funding Wallet
4. Select USDT

👉 Required for P2P

---

# 🧩 STEP 6: Go to P2P SELL

1. Open **P2P Trading**
2. Click **SELL**
3. Select:
    - Coin: USDT
    - Currency: INR

---

# 🧩 STEP 7: Choose buyer (VERY IMPORTANT)

Look for:

- ✅ 95%+ completion rate
- ✅ Verified merchant
- ✅ High orders

👉 This reduces scam risk

---

# 🧩 STEP 8: Enter amount (e.g., 100 USDT)

1. Enter amount
2. Click **Sell USDT**

---

# 🧩 STEP 9: WAIT for payment

Now:

- Buyer sees your UPI
- Sends money

👉 Timer starts (usually ~15 mins)

---

# 🧩 STEP 10: VERIFY MONEY (CRITICAL)

Do NOT trust screenshot

👉 Check:

- Your bank app
- Actual balance

---

# 🧩 STEP 11: Release crypto

Once money received:

1. Click **Release**
2. Confirm

👉 Binance sends USDT to buyer
